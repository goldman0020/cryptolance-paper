CryptoLance — Complete User Guide for the Test Version

Test website: https://cryptolance001.bonto.run
Blockchain network: TRON Shasta Testnet
Wallet: TronLink
Test payment token: Mock USDT Tron (mUSDT)

Welcome to CryptoLance! This guide explains how to prepare your wallet, access the platform, register as an employer or freelancer, participate in projects, and follow the platform's smart-contract workflow.

CryptoLance is an experimental platform for global project-based work. It connects employers with freelancers for remote digital work and agreed local services. Its intended workflow uses smart contracts to manage project funds and support automated payment and arbitration processes.

Important: This is an experimental test version. Use test-network assets only. Some blockchain operations may remain unavailable or incomplete while the test environment is being configured. Test tokens have no monetary value and are not real USDT.

Part 1 — Prepare Your Wallet

Step 1: Install or Open TronLink

CryptoLance requires a compatible TRON wallet for its blockchain-related features.

You can use either:

Mobile: Open the website inside the TronLink app's DApp browser.

Desktop or laptop: Open the website in a browser with the TronLink extension installed.

Official website: https://www.tronlink.org/

If you already have TronLink, open your wallet and make sure you can access your account.

Never share your wallet recovery phrase or private key with anyone, including CryptoLance.

Step 2: Select Shasta Testnet

CryptoLance's test environment uses the Shasta Testnet, not TRON Mainnet.

Open TronLink and select Shasta Testnet. Make sure you are using the same wallet account when connecting to CryptoLance.

Do not use real USDT or send real funds to test the platform.

Part 5 — How Employers Use CryptoLance

Step 1: Publish a Project

From the employer dashboard, create an advertisement for your project.

Enter the requested information, such as:

Project title

Description and requirements

Delivery time

Price

Deadline

Category

Location preference, where applicable

Review the terms carefully before publishing.

Your advertisement will appear in the project section, where freelancers can review available opportunities.

Step 2: Review Freelancers

Freelancers can express interest and communicate with you through the platform.

Review their profiles and relevant information before selecting a freelancer.

Once you select and confirm a freelancer, the freelancer has 72 hours to confirm the project through their dashboard.

If the freelancer does not complete the required confirmation within that period, the project is automatically treated as rejected and cancelled. You will need to create and publish the project again if you still want to proceed.

Keep a copy of your project description and terms so you can republish them if necessary.

Step 3: Confirm the Required Funding

Before a project can proceed, the employer must have sufficient funds for the required contract amounts.

According to the platform's current rules, the employer's required amounts include:

The project contract value

The 5% platform commission

The applicable dispute fee or arbitration reserve

Sufficient TRX for any network fees paid directly by the wallet

The platform checks the required balance. If funds are insufficient, the process may pause and an automated notice may appear.

Review the amounts displayed by CryptoLance before proceeding.

Step 4: Complete the Funding and Approval Process

Follow the instructions in the dashboard and wallet pop-ups.

Some stages require you to grant permissions or approve blockchain transactions. The freelancer must also complete the required steps.

When the platform asks you to sign or approve a transaction, verify the request and follow the instructions. Do not repeatedly submit actions while a previous transaction is still processing.

Wait for the dashboard to display the relevant status before moving forward.

Step 5: Review the Delivered Work

After the freelancer submits the work, review the deliverables against the agreed project terms.

Use the platform's payment-approval workflow when the work is complete and ready for approval.

Payments and fund distributions are governed by the applicable smart-contract rules. Manual refunds are not available through the normal project workflow.

Part 6 — How Freelancers Use CryptoLance

Step 1: Find a Project

Open the project page and review available advertisements.

Choose a project that matches your skills and availability.

Review the description, price, deadline, delivery requirements, and other terms before contacting the employer.

You can communicate with the employer through the platform.

Step 2: Confirm a Selected Project Within 72 Hours

When an employer selects you and confirms the project, the project will appear in the Smart Contract section of your dashboard.

You have 72 hours to confirm whether you want to accept the project.

If you do not complete the required confirmation in time, the project is automatically treated as rejected and cancelled. The platform also records a negative rating consequence under its current rules.

Do not accept a project unless you understand the requirements and can meet the agreed terms.

Step 3: Understand the Required Amounts

Before the project can be activated, the freelancer must have sufficient funds for the amounts required by the platform.

The current rules require:

A 5% performance security deposit based on the contract value

A 5% platform commission

The applicable dispute fee or arbitration reserve

Sufficient TRX for network fees paid directly by the wallet

The performance deposit is intended to demonstrate commitment to the project. Under the normal successful-completion workflow, the applicable project payment is released and the deposit is returned according to the contract rules.

Check the required amounts displayed by CryptoLance before proceeding.

Step 4: Approve One Project at a Time

You may see multiple project offers from the same employer in your Smart Contract section.

Although you can approve several offers consecutively, CryptoLance recommends approving one project at a time.

After approving a project:

Open the accordion menu on the left side of your dashboard.

Use the platform's Refresh option to continue the permission process.

Follow the messages shown by CryptoLance.

Allow the employer time to complete their required permissions.

Wait for the project's funding status to be resolved before proceeding to another project from the same employer.

Subsequent projects may remain queued until the preceding project's status has been resolved.

Step 5: Allow the Contract to Be Activated

After the required confirmations and permissions have been completed, the project should progress to the funding and activation stage.

Follow the dashboard messages and wait for the status to update.

Once the project is active, carry out the agreed work and submit the deliverables through the appropriate workflow.

After successful delivery and employer approval, payment and deposit handling follow the applicable smart-contract rules.

Part 7 — Important Wallet and Transaction Instructions

These instructions apply to both employers and freelancers.

Wait After Every Blockchain Action

Whenever you click a button that initiates a blockchain interaction, allow up to three minutes for the operation to complete.

After your final confirmation, if a transaction has been submitted:

Do not press other action buttons.

Do not refresh the browser page.

Do not leave the website.

Do not log out.

Wait for the page to update or for a confirmation message.

This waiting period gives the platform time to communicate with the smart contract and retrieve the result.

A wallet approval or authorization may itself be an on-chain transaction and may require network resources.

Use the Platform's Refresh Option

When CryptoLance instructs you to refresh the dashboard, use the Refresh option in the accordion menu on the left side of the platform.

Do not confuse this with refreshing the entire browser page.

The platform's Refresh option is intended to help the dashboard retrieve updated information and continue the workflow.

If a Project's Status Looks Incorrect

A network interruption or delayed response can sometimes cause the dashboard to display a status that does not yet reflect the actual smart-contract state.

If a project appears cancelled during the funding process:

Do not immediately repeat the original transaction or attempt another action.

Use the dashboard's Refresh option to retrieve the latest status.

Allow two to three minutes between refresh attempts.

If necessary, repeat the process up to three times while waiting for updated information.

If the project was genuinely cancelled because a required signature, permission, or sufficient balance was missing, the cancellation cannot be reversed through the normal workflow. The employer must republish the project if they wish to proceed.

If the status remains unclear, stop and report the issue rather than repeatedly submitting transactions.

Part 8 — Messages and Communication

CryptoLance provides inbox and communication features for employers and freelancers.

Use these features to:

Clarify project requirements.

Discuss progress and deliverables.

Communicate about delays or disagreements.

Keep relevant project discussions available for review.

Check your dashboard and inbox regularly, especially when a project requires your confirmation or another action.

If a dispute is referred to arbitration, the assigned arbitrator may access relevant project communications, records, and evidence needed to assess the dispute.

Part 9 — Dispute Resolution and Arbitration

CryptoLance supports an arbitration workflow when a project disagreement is referred for a decision.

Step 1: Refer the Project to Arbitration

If either party refers the project to arbitration through their dashboard, both parties will be able to see the referral in their respective dashboards.

Check your inbox to identify the arbitrator assigned automatically by the platform.

Step 2: Submit Evidence

Open the arbitration panel available in your dashboard.

Submit any supporting evidence you want the arbitrator to consider. The current dispute interface supports attachments such as PDF, DOC, DOCX, TXT, JPG, and PNG files, subject to the displayed file-size limit.

Provide a clear explanation of your position and include relevant evidence.

Step 3: Communicate Before the Ruling

Both parties may continue communicating before the arbitrator issues a decision.

If you reach a mutual agreement, both parties can submit the agreed terms through their respective arbitration panels. The arbitrator can then issue a ruling based on that agreement.

Step 4: Understand the Financial Outcome

After the arbitrator issues a ruling, the smart contract distributes the relevant funds according to the applicable decision and contract rules.

The arbitration fee is handled automatically under the contract rules, even if the parties reach an agreement after referral.

Do not assume that a manual refund is available. Review the contract's applicable outcome and wait for the platform to display the resulting status.

Part 10 — Fees and Funds: Important Information

CryptoLance's current About Site describes the following fee structure.

Platform Commission

The current platform commission is 5% of the contract value.

Dispute Fee Structure

The current published schedule is:

Projects under $500: $10

$501–$10,000: 8%

$10,001–$50,000: 6%

$50,001–$100,000: 4%

Above $100,000: 2%

Check the amounts shown by the platform before starting a project. The published schedule and the amounts calculated for a particular project should be reviewed carefully, especially for small test projects.

Refund and Arbitration Rules

Under the current published rules:

If no dispute occurs, the applicable dispute reserve is refunded according to the contract rules.

The freelancer's performance deposit is returned according to the normal successful-completion rules.

If arbitration is required, 50% of the total dispute fees is collected as the arbitration service fee.

The final distribution depends on the arbitrator's ruling and the applicable contract rules.

The blockchain transaction history and the smart-contract state determine the on-chain outcome. A dashboard message alone should not be treated as proof of a completed transaction if its status is still uncertain.

Part 11 — Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need real USDT to test CryptoLance?

No. This test environment uses a mock token for testing. Do not send real USDT or other real funds to test the platform.

Do I have to add mUSDT manually to TronLink?

No. Manual addition is optional. It is useful if you want to see the test token in your wallet's asset list. CryptoLance's balance check and minting flow are separate from manually displaying the token.

Why do I need test TRX?

Test TRX is needed for network resources and fees when a transaction must be submitted by your wallet. Use the Shasta Faucet to obtain test TRX.

Why did CryptoLance ask me to approve a transaction?

Some workflow steps require wallet authorization or a blockchain transaction. Review each request carefully and approve only the action you intended to initiate.

Why is the process waiting?

Blockchain interactions and platform status updates can take time. Wait up to three minutes after a submitted action. Follow the platform's instructions and use its dashboard Refresh option when requested.

What happens if a freelancer does not confirm within 72 hours?

The project is automatically treated as rejected and cancelled. The employer must publish it again if they still want to proceed.

What if the project is referred to arbitration?

Both parties can see the referral, identify the assigned arbitrator through their inbox, and submit relevant evidence through the arbitration panel.

Can a completed blockchain transaction be manually refunded?

The normal CryptoLance workflow does not support manual refunds. Fund distribution follows the applicable smart-contract rules and project outcome.
