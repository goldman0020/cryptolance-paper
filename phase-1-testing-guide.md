CryptoLance — Phase 1 Public Testing Guide

Welcome to CryptoLance!

Website: https://cryptolance001.bonto.run

CryptoLance has entered its first public testing phase!

CryptoLance is a blockchain-based platform designed to connect employers and freelancers across borders and support project-based work of different kinds—from remote digital services to physical, location-based projects.

After approximately 15 months of intensive, self-funded development and hundreds of successful tests, CryptoLance is now inviting the public to explore the platform, experience its features, and contribute feedback.

Phase 1 is an opportunity to interact with CryptoLance firsthand, discover how its different sections work, and help shape the next stage of development.

1. What Can You Explore in Phase 1?

Phase 1 focuses on exploring the platform's interface, registration, profiles, dashboards, project listings, communication, ratings, and wallet-related interactions.

You can explore how to:

Sign up, log in, log out, and navigate the platform.

Create and complete your profile.

Explore your dashboard and its different sections.

Check your displayed rating.

Create project advertisements as an employer.

Browse project listings and explore how different projects are presented.

Explore both physical and non-physical project opportunities.

Express interest in a project as a freelancer and send a message to its employer.

Use project-related chat to communicate with the other party.

Check automatic inbox messages, notifications, and project updates.

Visit your dashboard regularly to follow activity and messages.

Explore wallet connections, electronic signatures, permission requests, and balance-related notices.

Learn how to obtain test TRX, switch your wallet to the Shasta Testnet, and prepare it for test-environment interactions.

Understand the wallet balance requirements displayed for projects and how they relate to project amounts and platform rules.

Try Both Sides of the Marketplace

You do not need another person to explore CryptoLance.

You can sign up and experience the platform as a freelancer, then explore it as an employer through the available registration and account options. This allows you to see how projects, messages, dashboards, ratings, and interactions appear from both perspectives.

Trying both roles is one of the best ways to understand how CryptoLance is designed to connect employers and freelancers.

2. Get Started

Step 1 — Open the website

Visit:

https://cryptolance001.bonto.run

CryptoLance is currently available on the TRON Shasta Testnet for public testing.

Step 2 — Connect your wallet

CryptoLance supports wallet-based interactions through a compatible TRON wallet such as TronLink.

You can use a desktop browser with the TronLink extension or the TronLink DApp browser on a compatible mobile device.

Step 3 — Switch to the Shasta Testnet and obtain test TRX

For wallet-related testing, your wallet needs to be connected to the TRON Shasta Testnet.

You can request test TRX from the Shasta faucet:

https://shasta.tronex.io/join/getJoinPage

Open the faucet, enter your Shasta wallet address, and follow its instructions to request test TRX.

The faucet has advertised 2,000 test TRX per request, although availability and amounts may change.

For detailed instructions on switching networks, checking your balance, and optionally displaying CryptoLance's virtual mUSDT token in TronLink, see the Comprehensive CryptoLance Guide published alongside this document.

🔗 [Read the Comprehensive CryptoLance Guide here](https://github.com/goldman0020/cryptolance-paper/blob/main/user-guide-test-version.md)

Step 4 — Sign up and complete your profile

Follow the registration steps, complete your profile, and open your dashboard.

Explore the available menus and familiarize yourself with the way CryptoLance organizes your account, projects, messages, and ratings.

3. Explore CryptoLance as an Employer

Start by exploring the employer experience.

Open the relevant employer dashboard section.

Create a project advertisement using the available fields.

Explore the options for describing the project.

Consider how different types of work can be represented.

View your project in the project listings.

Open the project page and explore its dropdown menus and available options.

Check your dashboard and inbox for messages and notifications.

CryptoLance is designed to support a broad range of project-based work, including remote digital services and physical services such as inspection, installation, maintenance, driving, cleaning, cooking, and on-site supervision.

Explore how different kinds of work can be described and presented through the same platform.

4. Explore CryptoLance as a Freelancer

Now explore the freelancer experience.

Browse available project advertisements.

Open projects and review their descriptions.

Explore the available option for expressing interest in a project.

Send a message to the employer through the relevant project communication feature.

Return to your dashboard to check your inbox and notifications.

Explore how project conversations are organized.

Project-related communication is associated with the relevant project, helping both parties follow their conversations in context.

If you have already explored the employer experience, return to that role and observe how the project and communication features appear from the other side.

5. Explore Messages, Ratings, and Dashboard Activity

The dashboard is an important place to follow your activity on CryptoLance.

As you explore the platform, return to your dashboard regularly to check:

Automatic inbox messages and notifications.

Project-related messages and two-way communication.

Project activity and updates.

Your displayed rating.

Other information and options available for your role.

Explore the available menus and dropdowns, and try the relevant interface features. Some parts of the platform become easier to understand when you follow a project from the perspectives of both the employer and the freelancer.

6. Understand Wallet Interactions

CryptoLance integrates wallet interactions into its user workflows. Phase 1 provides an opportunity to become familiar with how these interactions are presented.

You can explore:

Connecting your wallet to the platform.

Switching to the Shasta Testnet and checking the active network.

Obtaining test TRX and checking your wallet balance.

Reviewing electronic transaction-signature requests.

Understanding wallet permission requests.

Reading balance-related messages and notices about the amount required for a project.

Understanding how the platform communicates the relationship between project amounts, wallet balances, and applicable requirements.

These interactions help you become familiar with the wallet-related steps that form part of the CryptoLance experience.

For detailed wallet setup instructions, including the optional virtual mUSDT token, consult the Comprehensive CryptoLance Guide.

🔗 [Read the Comprehensive CryptoLance Guide here](https://github.com/goldman0020/cryptolance-paper/blob/main/user-guide-test-version.md)

7. What Comes in the Next Phase?

The first phase is intended to help the public explore CryptoLance's interface and its main user-facing workflows.

The next phase will extend public testing to the smart-contract workflows for freezing and releasing project funds, dispute referral and arbitration, and related post-arbitration processes.

These core workflows have already undergone extensive testing, including hundreds of successful tests. The complementary phase will make them available for further testing in the broader public environment.

Additional hosting options will also be explored to support continued testing and improve access to the platform.

8. Your Experience and Feedback Matter

CryptoLance has been developed around the idea that access to work and opportunities should not be limited by borders or distance.

Public testing is an opportunity to experience what has been built, discover how the platform works in practice, and help improve its usability.

You are invited to:

Explore the platform at your own pace.

Try the employer and freelancer experiences.

Create and browse projects.

Explore dashboards, ratings, inbox messages, and project communication.

Become familiar with the wallet-related workflows.

Share your experience, suggestions, and constructive feedback.
